Sneak Peek

A first look at the newest arc — and what's coming next on this site.

🐉

Arc 4 has started

The Forgotten Isles Prophecy opened with The Hybrid Prince in March 2026, and two more books are planned. Two MudWings flee Jade Mountain Academy after a tragedy and end up on the Dungeon Isle, where a hybrid dragon named Mulberry pulls them out of the sea.

The Court of Refuge is nothing like Pyrrhia. It was built and enchanted by the animus dragon Waterfall, and twenty-five metal SharpWings — created by Precipice more than three thousand years ago — guard it. They record the name of every prisoner who enters, along with a category number, and stop anyone trying to cross the Barrier.

🧩

Who's already on the Island

Beryl is a SkyWing queen and herbalist who once advised Scarlet and Firestorm, and she used to rule the Dungeon Isle with her partner Snakeroot — a LeafWing–NightWing hybrid with foresight. Their son Mulberry is the hybrid prince himself: SkyWing, LeafWing and NightWing all at once, and the only dragon who can cross the Barrier.

But the rule is changing. Beryl and Snakeroot are now prisoners of the Court of Refuge, and the WildWings who live there are run by the elected Triumvirate. Aurora, an IceWing–SkyWing hybrid who breathes blue fire, sings at the Northern Lights concerts with her drummer brother Borealis — and Borealis was working secretly with a group of rebel dragons to overthrow the throne.
