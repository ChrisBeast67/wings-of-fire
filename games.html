The Games

Two playable mini-games from Pyrrhia. No downloads, no installs — just tap and go.

🐉 Dragon Flight

Fly a dragonet through the jungle pillars. Every gap you pass is a point. Don't hit anything.

Score0
Best0
Press start, or hit Space, to take flight.
🎮 Space / ↑ / click / tap to flap

🕹️ How to play

Tap, click or press Space to flap upward. Let go and you drop. Thread the gaps between the stone pillars — each pillar you clear scores a point, and the gaps get tighter as you go.

🏆 Your record

Your best flight is saved in this browser, so it stays with you between visits. Beat it, then beat it again.

🔥 Speed run

The pillars come faster the longer you survive. Ten points is a good flight. Twenty is a legend. Everything past thirty is Clay-level heroics.

🧩 Tribe Match

Memory game. Flip two cards and match the dragonets to their tribes — all ten, from SeaWing to WildWing.

Find all ten tribe pairs. Fewest moves wins.

🧠 How to play

Click a card to flip it and reveal the dragonet. Find its partner — the same tribe — to lock the pair in. Clear the whole board in as few moves as you can.

🐾 The ten tribes

SeaWing, MudWing, SandWing, SkyWing, NightWing, IceWing, RainWing, SilkWing, HiveWing and WildWing. Still not sure about the differences? The tribe quiz will tell you which one you are.
