The Characters

Heroes, villains, and the dragons in between.

The Five Dragonets

🐢

Clay

MudWing

Big, gentle and stubborn, and the first of the dragonets to hatch. Clay has massive, strong brown wings, thick mud-colored scales and fireproof scales — MudWings hatched from blood-red eggs, like Clay, are immune to fire. He nearly goes through with a murder plot before his friends talk him down — and he grows into one of Pyrrhia's greatest heroes.

🦎

Tsunami

SeaWing

Hot-headed, fearless and strong. Stolen from the Royal Hatchery as an egg, Tsunami returns to the Kingdom of the Sea to face a mother who never stopped loving her and a kingdom that wants her dead. She is the only dragonet who can breathe underwater.

🌧️

Glory

RainWing

Elegant, vain, deadly — and secretly the kindest of them all. Raised by the RainWings of the Rainforest Kingdom, Glory was always sharper and more venomous than the lazy stereotype of her tribe. After the NightWing Exodus she becomes queen of both the NightWings and the RainWings, a decision many NightWings still resent.

🦇

Starflight

NightWing

Smart, gentle and endlessly curious. The only dragonet who can read human language, which is how they learn the prophecy was rigged. Blinded in the volcanic eruption on the NightWing island, he still maps the world by scent and sound — and later becomes Jade Mountain Academy's head librarian.

🌵

Sunny

SandWing–NightWing hybrid

Small, fiery, and the bravest dragon in the group. Daughter of SandWing queen Thorn and the animus dragon Stonemover, Sunny grows up in the SandWing palace with golden wings, tiny scales and an enormous heart. She is the smallest of the dragonets of destiny, and she co-founds Jade Mountain Academy and never stops inventing.

Jade Mountain Academy & Arc 2

🌙

Moonwatcher

NightWing

A quiet NightWing who hatched under two full moons, giving her both mind-reading and prophecy — powers the NightWings hadn't had for nearly a century. Hidden in the rainforest by her mother Secretkeeper, Moon has to decide how much power is worth taking.

❄️

Winter

IceWing

An IceWing prince who is a disappointment to his royal family. When his sister Icicle runs away from Jade Mountain Academy, Winter sets out after her to make things right — and discovers the terrible secret at the heart of his own past.

🦋

Peril

SkyWing

A SkyWing with firescales, the most dangerous condition a dragon can have — and Scarlet's champion in the arena. When Scarlet turns on the Academy, Peril has to find out whether she can be more than the deadliest dragon in Pyrrhia.

🌊

Turtle

SeaWing

A SeaWing prince with secret animus magic who would rather read about heroes than be one. In Talons of Power he watches Darkstalker charm everyone at the Academy and knows that someone has to stop him.

🏜️

Qibli

SandWing

A wry, quick-witted SandWing from the Outclaws who becomes the protagonist of Darkness of Dragons. Without real animus magic, Qibli has to out-think the most dangerous dragon in history using nothing but a few borrowed objects.

🌈

Kinkajou

RainWing

Bouncy, fierce and permanently one degree too fast. Kinkajou was a captive of the NightWings on the island before ending up in the Jade Mountain winglet — and she still carries the marks of that captivity.

Pantala & Arc 3

🧵

Blue

SilkWing

A bright blue SilkWing, raised in Cicada Hive by Burnet and her mate Silverspot, who is perfectly happy where he is — right up until his half-sister Luna's wings start to grow. Blue is a flamesilk and a fugitive who was once controlled by the Othermind before escaping its power.

🐝

Cricket

HiveWing

A yellow-and-gold HiveWing who grows up asking questions nobody wants answered: why are trees forbidden, and why is she immune to Queen Wasp's mind control? The protagonist of The Hive Queen, and the audience's guide to Pantala.

🌿

Sundew

LeafWing

A LeafWing warrior with a powerful leafspeak ability, raised by the PoisonWings — her mother Belladonna leads them — in the jungle of Pantala. Sundew has spent her life wanting to destroy the HiveWings, and the Poison Jungle holds the secret that could let her do it.

❄️

Snowfall

IceWing

Young queen of the IceWings with a crown too heavy for her head and a plan to keep everyone else out of her kingdom. Then a storm of strange dragons arrives from the ocean, and Snowfall has to decide who she really is.

🧶

Luna

SilkWing

Blue's half-sister, a green SilkWing dragonet who can spin flamesilk. Luna always wanted to change the world; in The Flames of Hope she finally gets the chance to try.

🐝

Queen Wasp

HiveWing

The former queen of the Hives. Her power came not from her blood but from the Othermind — a sentient plant that let her control the minds of nearly every HiveWing. Cricket, Scarab and most of the Hive rulers were immune, and that is enough to cost Wasp her throne.

Villains & Legends

💀

Darkstalker

NightWing–IceWing hybrid

Ancient. Powerful. Possibly the most dangerous dragon in Pyrrhia's history. Hatched with animus magic, mind-reading and prophecy on the brightest night, he schemes his way out of the lost city of night and enchants whole tribes — until he spitefully eats a strawberry enchanted by Kinkajou, which strips his memories and his powers and leaves him as Peacemaker.

🔴

Scarlet

SkyWing

The SkyWing queen for nearly thirty years, challenged fourteen times and never once losing. She used Peril's firescales in her arena, ruled through fear during the war, and was finally killed by her own daughter in a royal challenge.

🌑

Mastermind

NightWing

Starflight's father and the NightWings' head scientist, a whip-thin genius with bloodshot eyes who performed terrible experiments on the RainWings. Starflight's mother is Farsight, and Mastermind is also Fierceteeth's stepfather. He lives on in the Rainforest Kingdom, in prison.

🕷️

Morrowseer

NightWing

The NightWing who helped fabricate the false Dragonet Prophecy with Queen Battlewinner, and one of her most trusted advisors. He came to the Combe to inspect the dragonets and ordered Glory killed. He was also Moonwatcher's father, though Secretkeeper kept Moon a secret from him — and he was killed in the volcanic eruption on the NightWing island.

🪶

Fatespeaker

NightWing

One of the false dragonets raised in place of Starflight, with a hopeful heart and the faintest hint of foresight. She can sense vague emotions from other dragons, has romantic feelings for Starflight, and works as an assistant librarian at Jade Mountain Academy.

🦔

Fierceteeth

NightWing

Starflight's maternal half-sister, certain she could have been a dragonet of destiny and bitter about it. The protagonist of the first Winglet, Prisoners, she is now the self-proclaimed leader of the settlement of Renewal.

Queens of Pyrrhia

🔵

Queen Coral

SeaWing

Queen of the SeaWings and mother of Tsunami. Coral is a writer whose scrolls are required reading in SeaWing schools, and she rules with genuine affection for her subjects.

🟠

Queen Thorn

SandWing

Sunny's mother and the SandWing queen chosen during The Brightest Night, after the Eye of Onyx settles the succession. Small, wiry, and absolutely immovable.

🟡

Queen Glory

NightWing & RainWing

After the NightWing island's volcano erupts, the NightWings crown Glory — a RainWing — as their queen, and she ends up ruling both tribes. Her queenship is beloved by some and deeply resented by others.

🟣

Queen Battlewinner

NightWing

The NightWing queen of the island era, described in the dragon guides as "a closely guarded secret." Mother of Greatness, she froze to death trying to attack Glory and Starflight.

🔷

Queen Glacier

IceWing

The IceWing queen who fell victim to the fatal IceWing plague spread by Darkstalker. Her death left the IceWing throne in chaos: her daughter Crystal swore she would never challenge the chosen princess, and her other daughter Snowfall took the crown instead.

💎

Queen Ruby

SkyWing

The current queen of the SkyWings — the dragon who challenged Scarlet and won the throne. She is one of the three heroes of the Winglet novella Hero, alongside her son Cliff and her partner Jasper. Ruby's mother is Queen Scarlet, and her grandmother is Queen Beryl — making Cliff Beryl's great-grandson.

Winglets & Supporting Cast

🗡️

Deathbringer

NightWing

An apprentice assassin whose mother is Quickstrike, the NightWings' deadliest killer. The star of the Winglet novella Assassin — and now the partner of Queen Glory.

🐾

Six-Claws

SandWing

A loyal SandWing general who deserts Princess Burn's army during the war and ends up in the Scorpion Den. Today he is Queen Thorn's most trusted general.

❄️

Arctic & Foeslayer

IceWing & NightWing

Prince Arctic of the IceWings and Foeslayer the NightWing, the lovers whose elopement started a tribal rivalry that lasted thousands of years — and made them the parents of Darkstalker.

🐢

Silver

Sloth

Glory's royal pet: a silver-furred sloth who was fast asleep on top of Glory when she woke up from her very first suntime. She never says a word, but she seems to enjoy spending time with Sunny — and Sunny adores her.

🐉

Sky

SkyWing

Peril's twin brother: a small SkyWing who hatched unable to breathe fire. Son of Chameleon in his Soar form and Kestrel, and the SkyWing who befriends the human Wren in the Legend Dragonslayer.

🦅

Kestrel

SkyWing

A member of the Talons of Peace and one of the dragonets' guardians, teaching them the strengths and weaknesses of every tribe. Blister murdered her shortly after leaving her daughter Peril behind.

🍓

Peacemaker

NightWing–RainWing hybrid

What Darkstalker became. Foeslayer — later known as Hope — first tried to talk Darkstalker into eating a strawberry to wipe his memory, but it was Kinkajou who used scraps of Darkstalker's scroll to enchant one. The ancient dragon ate it anyway — and shrank into a young dragonet with none of his memories or powers. Now raised by his mother Hope in the NightWing village, he loves strawberries and is writing the tribe-famous song "Strawberries as Big as a Scavenger's Head."

The Forgotten Isles Prophecy — Arc 4

The newest arc, and the first of a planned three-book arc, takes place on the Dungeon Isle and the Court of Refuge.

🟤

Umber

MudWing

The protagonist of The Hybrid Prince. Umber is Clay's younger brother — one of seven MudWing siblings hatched from eggs laid by the same mother, Cattail — and he has spent his whole life in Clay's shadow. When a tragedy at Jade Mountain sends him fleeing to the Dungeon Isle, he discovers he is braver than he ever believed.

🐍

Sora

MudWing

Umber's sister, and one of Clay's siblings. Shy, quiet and painfully introverted, Sora has spent years grieving her sister Crane, who was killed by Icicle in the war. Her attempts to get even end in tragedy at Jade Mountain, and she flees to the Dungeon Isle to train as a healer with Paua.

🍂

Mulberry

SkyWing–LeafWing–NightWing hybrid

Kind, shy and responsible, Mulberry is the son of Queen Beryl and Snakeroot, and the former prince of the Court of Refuge. He can read and alter the emotions of living beings with dragonspeak, and he is one of the very few dragons who can cross the Barrier because he descends from both Queen Summit and Queen Retribution. He has romantic feelings for Umber.

👑

Queen Beryl

SkyWing

Ruby's grandmother, Cliff's great-grandmother, and the mother of both Canyon and Mulberry. Beryl came to the Court of Refuge to claim the throne outright, dismissing its strange ritual of voting and rotating power as un-dragonlike — and ended up imprisoned by the very court she tried to conquer.

🌙

Snakeroot

LeafWing–NightWing hybrid

Queen Beryl's partner and Mulberry's father, a powerful LeafWing–NightWing hybrid with foresight. He came to the Court of Refuge with Beryl to take the throne, and when their plan failed, he ordered his King's Teeth to kill the Triumvirate.

🎵

Aurora

IceWing–SkyWing hybrid

An IceWing–SkyWing hybrid who can breathe blue fire and sing with an ethereal voice, Aurora is the Court's most famous performer, headlining the Northern Lights concerts with her brother Borealis on drums. Her songs give the dragons of the Dungeon Isle hope — and her quiet rebellion against the crown is why Beryl imprisoned her for helping Mulberry escape.

⚖️

The Triumvirate

WildWing, SkyWing–SeaWing & SilkWing

The three elected dragons who rule the Court of Refuge in peaceful rotation: Tuatara, an elderly WildWing; Horizon, a young SkyWing–SeaWing hybrid; and Monarch, a SilkWing of at least seventy years. The Court's rule by consent is exactly what Queen Beryl came to destroy.

💧

Waterfall & Precipice

SeaWing & SkyWing

Two of the great animus dragons of Pyrrhia's past. Waterfall, a SeaWing, created the Court of Refuge itself along with its orchards and never-dry water sources, while Precipice, a SkyWing, cast the prison spells and created the twenty-five metal SharpWing sentinels that still guard it. Both are long dead, but their enchantments still hold.

Why These Characters Work

The reason people fall for Pyrrhia is usually the same: nobody here is a cardboard cut-out.

💔

They're flawed, not broken

Clay wants to be a hero and nearly murders a queen. Glory is vain and hides it as confidence. Sunny is desperate to be enough. Peril is dangerous on purpose, because nobody gave her another option. The flaws are the point — that's where the growth happens.

🕯️

The villains are the best part

Queen Wasp, Queen Battlewinner, Morrowseer, the Othermind itself — the great antagonists are usually characters with a point of view you can almost agree with. Scarlet was a queen who lost her way; Cottonmouth, one of the three minds woven into the Othermind, genuinely believed Pantala was stolen from him. That's harder to fight than a monster.

🤝

The bonds do the heavy lifting

Blue and Cricket. Umber and Sora. Fierceteeth and Strongwings. Moonwatcher and his secret. Every arc adds a new pair you'd follow anywhere — and the tender moments land harder because you've seen what these dragons almost lost.
