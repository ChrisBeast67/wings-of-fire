The Characters

Heroes, villains, and the dragons in between.

The Five Dragonets

🐢

Clay

MudWing

Big, gentle and stubborn. Clay's shell is as strong as oak, he can't fly and he desperately wants to. He almost goes through with Queen Asteria's murder plot before his friends talk him down. Later he becomes a guard captain, a hero of the War of Succession and the beloved big brother of the MudWing palace.

🦇

Glory

NightWing

Elegant, vain, deadly — and secretly the kindest of them all. Glory grew up as a captive sand sculpture in the palace, escaped on her own, and became the best NightWing flier alive. She doesn't want a crown, and she'd rather fight than talk.

🐦

Starflight

SkyWing

Smart, gentle and endlessly curious. Starflight was the first dragonet to read human books, which is how the dragonets learned the prophecy was fake. He keeps his own secrets, too — some of them are big.

🌵

Sunny

SandWing

Small, fiery, and the bravest dragon in the group. Sunny was born during the eruption of the volcano and raised in the palace. She can't fly, hates being helpless, and eventually becomes queen of the SandWings. Also: great inventor.

🦎

Dragonet

MudWing

The one nobody talks about. Dragonet is the sixth dragonet raised in the Combe — the one the prophecy never mentioned. Nobody knows what she looks like, and the dragonets are prepared to give up their memories of her to save the world.

Jade Mountain Academy & Arc 2

🌙

Moonwatcher

NightWing

A quiet NightWing who discovers she can talk to dragons telepathically — and then to an ancient animus dragon nobody else can hear. Moon wants to be a queen like her grandmother, and she has to decide how much power is worth taking.

❄️

Winter

IceWing

Big, sharp-tongued and hiding a huge secret: he was the hatchling who caused the IceWing fire that split his family apart. Winter comes to Jade Mountain to find his lost sister — and to make up for the worst day of his life.

🔥

Cinder

FireWing

Small, red and furious, Cinder is adopted by Peril and then betrays her for the sake of the academy. She's mean, insecure and completely loyal to the dragons she actually loves.

🦋

Peril

SkyWing

A beautiful SkyWing with a rare talent for changing the size of her scales. Peril is terrified of the MudWing emperor she was supposed to marry, and her journey from prisoner to Jade Mountain dragon is one of the best arcs in the series.

🟡

Ember

FireWing

Cinder's older brother, huge and dim and endlessly cheerful. Ember is the living proof that you don't have to be clever to be the best dragon in the room.

💀

Darkstalker

NightWing

Ancient. Powerful. Possibly the most dangerous dragon in Pyrrhia's history. Trapped under Jade Mountain for centuries, he scheme his way out, enchant entire tribes, and finally falls to the most embarrassing defeat in dragon history: a poisoned strawberry.

Arc 3 & Beyond

🦗

Cricket

NightWing

A NightWing warrior from the Island of Dragons who turns out to be much more than she seems. Cricket is the audience's guide to Pantala — and she is absolutely not a spy.

🌙

Moon

SandWing

Moonwatcher, all grown up: queen of the SandWings, and the dragon who faces the Silverwing Queen's mind-control song head-on. She loses the battle of wills. She does not lose her mind.

🐦

Silverwing

SkyWing

An old SkyWing who knows more about the Silverwing queens than anyone. She's the one who tells Copper the truth about the mind-control song — and what it costs.

🐝

Copper

HiveWing

A brass HiveWing born with no wings, which means he's supposed to be a worker drone. Instead he becomes a spy, a warrior, and the dragon who learns the Silverwing Queen's most dangerous secret.

🌊

Lantern

SeaWing

A young SeaWing with big dreams, a tiny body and a very big heart. Lantern wants to prove himself to the Coral Kingdom — and he's the bravest one in the group.

👑

Queen Wasp

HiveWing

The Silverwing Queen of the Hive. She rules with a song that empties a dragon of everything but obedience. She's smart, patient, and utterly ruthless.

Queens & Bosses of Pyrrhia

🧡

Queen Bronze

MudWing

Clay's mother and the queen of the Marsh. Tough, fair and completely unmovable — she makes Clay's brother Ripple heir instead of him, and Clay never quite forgives her.

🟤

Queen Asteria

MudWing

Bronze's mother, the villain of Arc 1. She rules through the MudWing secret of reading mud, and she nearly starts a war over it.

🟡

Queen Marva

NightWing

The NightWing queen who rules through mind-reading and knows every secret in her kingdom. She is also the one hiding a dragonet's true identity under the sand.

🟢

Queen Claribel

NightWing

Marva's mother and the queen whose murder starts the whole NightWing succession mess. Sometimes the real villain is just a victim of the last one.

🩵

Queen Silvy

SandWing

Sunny's mother. A SandWing queen who was framed for the war and died in the palace, her true self hidden in the shape of a small MudWing girl called NoSuchDragon. The best twist in the series.

🔺

Scorpion

SandWing

Red, furious and convinced the prophecy belongs to her. Scorpion is the Talon of Power who will do anything — including murder — to be queen.

Winglets & Legends

🦇

Nyx

NightWing

An assassin with a tragic backstory and a very sharp knife. She's the star of the Winglet novella Assassin.

🌵

Scarlet

SandWing

A palace prisoner and spy, watching the war from inside the walls in Prisoners.

🐦

Stormfly

SkyWing

The Swiftwing queen and her runaway sister Ivy, in the Winglet Runaway.

🐉

Goringtail

MudWing

The legendary dragon-slaying MudWing of the Dragonslayer Legend — the only dragon to survive the ancient dragon-hunting era.
