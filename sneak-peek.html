The Forgotten Isles Prophecy

Arc 4 has its own prophecy — and this one has a genuine mystery at its centre.

✦ The Forgotten Isles Prophecy ✦

Dragons lost and long forgotten
Abandoned in the sunlit isle
Ruled by power and betrayal
Haunted by an ancient trial.

Lines of blood will hold the key
As danger threatens from the past
And when the claws are at his throat
The hybrid prince will rise at last.

Who springs the trap, who saves the queens,
And which of them is friend or foe?
The smallest claws must choose the path
To right a wrong from long ago.

But is it wise and is it safe
To break the curse, unleash the tide?
What happens when the walls come down,
If something else was trapped inside?

Given by Snakeroot in The Hybrid Prince
🔓

The Lost and the Forgotten

The "dragons lost and long forgotten" are the prisoners of the Dungeon Isle — dragons hiding from their own pasts. The "ancient trial" they are haunted by is the Memory Room, where every prisoner has to face what they did.

🩸

Lines of Blood

"Lines of blood will hold the key" — the key to the island is inheritance. Only a dragon descended from both Queen Summit and Queen Retribution can cross the Barrier. Exactly one dragon currently can: Mulberry.

🐾

The Smallest Claws

"The smallest claws must choose the path." That is Umber — the youngest sibling of his MudWing hatching, and the one who was never supposed to be a hero. The prophecy hands the story to the character with the least claim to it.

Worth knowing: the poem is deliberately ambiguous, and the community's interpretations of it are not canon. Everything the prophecy actually states is in the lines above — everything else is a guess until the books say otherwise.

New Dragons to Meet

The Court of Refuge is full of surprises. Here is who lives there now.

🦋

Harmony

BeetleWing · LeafWing · RainWing · SeaWing

A hybrid dragonet who hatched from one of the two eggs enchanted by Precipice. She was named after the ancient village of the same name — and she is only two months old.

🌊

Horizon

SkyWing · SeaWing hybrid

At most twenty years old, with dappled red and blue scales, and he can breathe fire. One of the three Triumvirate ruling the Court.

🐝

Royal

HiveWing · SilkWing hybrid

A male hybrid whose grandmother is the current SilkWing queen, Monarch — one of the Triumvirate. His mother is Diadem, his father Glowworm.

⚙️

Nineteen

SharpWing

Around three thousand years old, created by Precipice. A guard of the Dungeon Isle who is now a caretaker of Harmony.

🌿

Precipice

The SkyWing animus dragon who enchanted the twenty-five SharpWings and the two eggs that could free the prisoners. She is roughly three thousand years old, and her magic still runs the Court of Refuge.

💧

Waterfall

The SeaWing animus dragon who built and enchanted the Court of Refuge itself, along with its orchards and its never-dry water sources. Her enchantment is what keeps the island alive.

🦅

The Triumvirate

Tuatara, the elderly WildWing · Horizon, the young SkyWing–SeaWing hybrid · Monarch, the SilkWing queen who is at least seventy years old. Elected — and they can be voted out.

Still To Come

Books, comics and the screen adaptation still on the way.

📕

Darkness of Dragons

Graphic novel · Dec 29, 2026

The comic adaptation of book ten, told from Qibli's point of view, with art by Mike Holmes. Qibli knows Darkstalker must be stopped — and he knows he could stop him, if he had even a sliver of the ancient dragon's scroll.

The Winglets Quintet

Graphic novel · May 4, 2027

All five Winglet stories in one comic, with art by Jake Parker: Prisoners, Assassin, Deserter, Runaway and Hero — plus a brand new story that has never been seen in print.

April 6, 2027

Mazes, search-and-finds, crosswords, trivia and word searches, plus an enormous 500-piece puzzle in a clever book-shaped box. Illustrated by Brianna C. Walsh.

Tiny coloring book · June 1, 2027

Over 45 dragons to colour, five mini markers and a backpack clip. Colour on the go.

Arc 4, Books 2 and 3

Coming · no dates yet

The Forgotten Isles Prophecy is planned as a three-book arc, and only the first has arrived. Six months after Umber finds the cave, Harmony hatches — and a month later, seven queens and several others vanish from Pyrrhia at once, and an unknown dragon steals the second egg. The next book up is book seventeen, starring Kinkajou — announced at FanWing Fest 2026, with no title or release date yet.

📕

Book 17 — starring Kinkajou

Coming · no dates yet

Tui Sutherland announced at FanWing Fest 2026 that the seventeenth main-series book will be Kinkajou's — the bouncy, snarky, fiercely loyal RainWing who was held captive in the Night Kingdom, enchanted the strawberry that turned Darkstalker into Peacemaker, and vanished alongside seven queens in the aftermath of The Hybrid Prince. No title, no cover and no release date yet.

📺

The Screen Adaptation

In development

The Netflix version was cancelled in May 2022 over creative differences. A new animated series is being developed by Amazon MGM Studios, with Aaron Waltke announced as showrunner in May 2025 and Marc Resteghini executive producing through Jack Tar Productions. It is a complete remake — nothing from the Netflix show can be reused.

Dates can move. Publishing dates for upcoming books are set by Scholastic and occasionally shift. Everything above comes from the publisher's own listings, and the series itself says to expect changes.
