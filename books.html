Where to start

Three ways in, depending on how much time you have.

🐢 One arc

Books 1–5 tell one complete story — five dragonets, one false prophecy, one very real ending. Read them in order and you'll understand everything that follows.

🌗 One book

Each main novel follows a different dragonet, so any one of them works alone. The Brightest Night is the most self-contained; Escaping Peril is the wildest ride.

🌟 The whole saga

Sixteen books, five Winglets, two Legends. Scholastic rates the series for ages 7–12, and it's been on the New York Times Best Seller list for over 300 weeks.

Screen Adaptations

The books have been in development for television more than once.

📺

Warner Bros. Animation / Netflix

Announced March 2020 · Cancelled May 2022

An animated series was announced for Netflix with ARRAY and Warner Bros. Animation, with Ava DuVernay attached to direct. Production began in April 2021 with a plan for ten 40-minute episodes. Netflix scrapped the project in May 2022, and the author received an enormous outpouring of fan anger.

📺

Amazon MGM Studios

Picked up February 2024

Amazon Prime Video took the books on with an entirely different creative team, with Jack Tar Productions and executive producer Marc Resteghini assigned to the adaptation. In May 2025, Aaron Waltke was announced as showrunner, co-writer and executive producer, with Tui Sutherland as co-writer and executive producer.

Graphic novel adaptations of the main series are drawn by Mike Holmes, while the Darkstalker graphic novel is illustrated by Jake Parker. The Winglets Quintet, a graphic novel collection of the five Winglets illustrated by Jake Parker and including a new print story, is due in the United States on May 4, 2027. Publication dates for newer titles follow the publishers' announced schedules.
