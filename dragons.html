The Dragon Tribes

Ten tribes. Ten kingdoms. One very messed-up world.

🌊

SeaWings

Proud, graceful and famously private, the SeaWings rule the Coral Kingdom under the Jade Palace. Their wings shimmer like the water, and their ceremonies are beautiful — but their pride is a weakness. They were the first tribe to be driven from Pyrrhia by humans, and they never forgot it.

💧 Ability: breathe underwater, shimmer their scales

🏝️ Home: The Coral Kingdom

🦎

MudWings

Big, warm and messy, the MudWings live in the Marsh of Perpetual Drizzle with their enormous families. They breathe underwater and can survive anything the swamp throws at them. Clay, the biggest dragonet, is a MudWing — and so is Bronze, the toughest queen in Pyrrhia.

💧 Ability: breathe underwater, huge strength

🌿 Home: The Marsh of Perpetual Drizzle

🌵

SandWings

Desert-dwellers with pale gold scales and a gift for staying cool. SandWings are quick, fierce and used to the desert's cruelty. Sunny and Scorpion are SandWings — and so are the war-hungry Talons of Power who start the SandWing succession war.

☀️ Ability: immunity to heat and sandstorms

🏜️ Home: The Kingdom of Sand

🐦

SkyWings

Bright, loud and gloriously free, the SkyWings rule the Sky Kingdom high above the mountains. Every SkyWing dreams of the Flame of Friendship, and the wings of a SkyWing are strong enough to carry a dragon through a storm.

🍃 Ability: fly, extra-strong wings

⛰️ Home: The Sky Kingdom

🌙

NightWings

Dark, elegant and secretive, the NightWings hide in the caves of the Island of Dragons. They can see in total darkness and prefer it. Their queen rules with a talent called mind-reading — the NightWing power of "seeing" in others' minds.

👁️ Ability: see in the dark, mind-reading (queens)

🦇 Home: The Cave of Despair

❄️

IceWings

Cold-blooded in the worst way — literally frozen, sparkly and pale blue. IceWings rule the Tens of Thousand Islands and can survive temperatures that would freeze any other dragon solid. Winter is the IceWing of Arc 2, and he's got a family mystery to solve.

🧊 Ability: immunity to cold and ice

🏔️ Home: The Tens of Thousand Islands

🔥

FireWings

Big, loud and literally on fire, the FireWings live among the Volcanoes of Sunrise and refuse to be bossed around. Their immunity to fire makes them fearless and unstoppable — and makes them excellent hunters.

🔥 Ability: immunity to fire

🌋 Home: The Volcanoes of Sunrise

🌧️

RainWings

Soft-spoken and famously hard to see, the RainWings blend into the rainforest of the Amazonian Basin. They can breathe underwater, change their scale size, and are so good at hiding that even other RainWings lose them.

💦 Ability: breathe underwater, scale-shifting camouflage

🌴 Home: The Rainforest (Amazonian Basin)

🌿

LeafWings

Newcomers to Pyrrhia — refugees who arrived from another land and were chased into the Leafscape. LeafWings are gentle, wise and can fly, and they turned the ruined Leafscape into a garden.

🍃 Ability: fly, plant-tending

🍃 Home: The Leafscape

🐝

HiveWings

Inventive, hard-working insect dragons from the continent of Pantala. HiveWings build silk and machines, and their queen rules with a hypnotic song that empties minds and fills them with obedience. Queen Wasp is the most dangerous dragon in the world.

🧠 Ability: silk production, mind-control song (queen)

🐛 Home: The Hive, Pantala

💀

The Court of Refuge

Not a tribe but a prison — the Court of Refuge on the island of Galar, where exiled dragons are locked away for life. Some of the oldest and most dangerous dragons in Pyrrhia are stuck there. Arc 4 is set here.

🔒 Ability: none — just centuries of grudges

🏝️ Home: Galar Island

World of Pyrrhia

The planet the dragons call home.

🌍 Pyrrhia

The main continent. Desert, jungle, swamp, mountains, rainforest and sky — nearly every tribe lives here, and it has been in turmoil for as long as anyone can remember.

🐛 Pantala

The second continent: a tropical jungle full of silk-producing insects, giant animals and the HiveWings. Only visited in Arc 3.

✨ Animus Magic

A rare genetic gift that lets a dragon move objects with their mind. Darkstalker had it. So does anyone else born with a green glow in their eyes.

🕯️ Humans

Dragonkind calls them animals. The humans of Pyrrhia have history with the dragons — including the human who started it all by stealing an egg.

🩸 Dragon-kinds of war

Thousands of years ago dragons burned human settlements to the ground. Two thousand years ago, a whole tribe was exiled to the Court of Refuge. Then, twenty years before Arc 1, a human killed the SandWing queen.

🔮 The Prophecy

"The dragonets will rise to unite the tribes and end the war... and one will destroy the world." Everyone assumed it was a joke. The Combe was supposed to hide them forever.
