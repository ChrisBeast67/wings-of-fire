The Dragon Tribes

Ten tribes. Two continents. One very messed-up world.

The Pyrrhian Tribes

Seven tribes on the continent of Pyrrhia — the seven named in the dragon guides.

🌊

SeaWings

Proud, graceful and famously private, the SeaWings rule the Kingdom of the Sea from the Deep Palace in the Bay of a Thousand Scales. Their claws are curved like fishhooks, their tails can batter opponents into waves, and their stripes glow in the dark. Queen Coral is a writer whose scrolls are required reading in SeaWing schools.

💧 Ability: breathe and see underwater, swim fast, glowing stripes

🏝️ Home: The Kingdom of the Sea (Bay of a Thousand Scales)

🦎

MudWings

Big, warm and messy, the MudWings live in the Mud Kingdom along the Diamond Spray Delta with their enormous families. They are immensely strong, can camouflage into mud and dirt, use mud to ease pain and treat wounds, and hold their breath for up to an hour. MudWings hatched from blood-red eggs are immune to fire — Clay is one of them.

💪 Ability: hold breath for an hour, breathe fire when warm, camouflage in mud

🌿 Home: The Mud Kingdom

🌵

SandWings

Desert-dwellers with scales that radiate warmth and pale gold skin, the SandWings rule the Kingdom of Sand. They are naturally adapted to heat and lack of water, can go for days with very little food, camouflage with sand, and have sharp hearing and smell. Their kingdom is vast and empty, and many live in oasis towns — including the notorious Scorpion Den, where merchants and assassins trade.

☀️ Ability: radiate heat, breathe fire, survive with almost no water, keen senses

🏜️ Home: The Kingdom of Sand

🐦

SkyWings

Bright, loud and gloriously free, the SkyWings rule the Sky Kingdom — the "wing" of Pyrrhia — high in the mountains and foothills of the Claws of the Clouds. They are the fastest fliers of any tribe thanks to their enormous wings and stamina, they are strong fighters, and they can breathe fire. Some carry firescales — Peril is the most famous SkyWing with them. The Sky Kingdom is now ruled by Queen Ruby, after the iron-fisted reign of Scarlet.

🍃 Ability: fastest fliers in Pyrrhia, extra-strong wings, fire breath

⛰️ Home: The Sky Kingdom

🌙

NightWings

Dark, elegant and secretive, the NightWings are a nocturnal tribe that can blend into the night sky and shadows, breathe fire, and have excellent night vision. Their real power is inheritance: dragons hatched under one full moon can read minds or see the future, and those hatched under two have both. Their old city on the Talon Peninsula was abandoned when Darkstalker murdered Prince Arctic — and over time the peninsula sank into the sea and shrank under earthquakes, avalanches, tidal waves and Darkstalker's own magic. They settled on a volcanic island north of Pyrrhia. When that island's volcano finally erupted in the NightWing Exodus, the tribe was sent to the Rainforest Kingdom, where most NightWings live today — though 27 who split away from Queen Glory now form the Renewal settlement, built among the ruins of the lost city of night.

👁️ Ability: vanish into shadow, night vision, mind-reading and prophecy (moon-hatched)

🦇 Home: the Rainforest Kingdom (formerly the NightWing island)

❄️

IceWings

Cold-blooded in the worst way — literally frozen, sparkly and pale blue. The IceWings rule the Ice Kingdom at the "head" of Pyrrhia, where the Great Ice Cliff once guarded the border — a wall Queen Snowfall has been tearing down piece by piece. They can withstand subzero temperatures and very bright light, have serrated claws, sharper eyesight than the other Pyrrhian tribes, and can exhale a freezing weapon. IceWings believe themselves the most superior of all dragon tribes, and their strict codes are famous.

🧊 Ability: withstand subzero cold and bright light, freezing breath

🏔️ Home: The Ice Kingdom

🌧️

RainWings

Soft-spoken and famously hard to see, the RainWings live an idyllic life in the Rainforest Kingdom. Their scales change color, they have long prehensile tails for swinging through the trees, long fangs and deadly venom, and they absorb sunlight for energy during "suntime". Other tribes stereotype them as lazy and ignorant — Glory proves them all wrong.

💧 Ability: color-changing scales, prehensile tail, venom, sunlight absorption

🌴 Home: The Rainforest Kingdom

The Pantalan Tribes

On the continent of Pantala live the silk-spinning insects and the forest dragons of the LeafSilk Kingdom.

🐝

HiveWings

Inventive, hard-working insect dragons from the continent of Pantala, the HiveWings live in great hives. Their powers vary from dragon to dragon: sharp stingers, deadly venom, a paralyzing nerve poison, boiling acid and a foul stench. Their former queen, Wasp, ruled through the Othermind — a sentient plant that let her control nearly every HiveWing mind. Cricket, Scarab and most of the Hive rulers were immune, and Cricket's immunity is the heart of The Hive Queen. Since the Othermind's destruction the HiveWings are ruled by Queen Jewel.

🧠 Ability: stingers, venom, nerve poison, boiling acid — the old queen's mind control came from the Othermind plant

🐛 Home: The Hives, Pantala

🧵

SilkWings

Industrious weavers of the LeafSilk Kingdom, the SilkWings are tree dwellers with sensitive antennae that let them sense vibrations in the air, predict the weather and feel danger coming. At six years old they undergo Metamorphosis: five days in a cocoon, emerging with two pairs of wings and silk glands in their wrists. Some can spin flamesilk, and the whole kingdom is run by the SilkWing Assembly rather than a single queen.

🧶 Ability: silk production, vibration senses, Metamorphosis at six, flamesilk

🕸️ Home: The LeafSilk Kingdom, Pantala

🌿

LeafWings

The LeafWings were originally a Pyrrhian tribe. They and the BeetleWings were framed for the assassination of the SkyWing queen Falcon — a plot arranged to hand RainWing, NightWing and SkyWing territory to the conspirators — and were banished from the Leaf Kingdom by Queen Summit as a result. They fled to Pantala, where they could never return to Pyrrhia, and settled in the LeafSilk Kingdom and the wild, dangerous Poison Jungle. LeafWings absorb energy from sunlight and some speak "leafspeak", convincing plants to grow, change shape, or even attack. Sundew is their great warrior of Arc 3.

🍀 Ability: sunlight absorption, gardening, leafspeak (plant speech)

🌿 Home: The LeafSilk Kingdom and Poison Jungle, Pantala

🦗

BeetleWings

BeetleWings were the first settlers of Pantala, arriving alongside the LeafWings as refugees from Pyrrhia to escape the trouble caused by Queen Falcon's assassination. They had four wings, were brightly colored, and were eventually bred into extinction after Clearsight came to Pantala, splitting into the HiveWings and the SilkWings. Today they are remembered as a legend of a tribe that no longer flies.

💀 Extinct — their story survives in the Legend of the Hive

❌ Home: none — gone from the world

The Dungeon Isle

🏝️

WildWings

The WildWings are descended from the first prisoners of the Dungeon Isle — LeafWings, BeetleWings, SeaWings and RainWings who could not escape — on a large island south of Pyrrhia that was enchanted three thousand years ago as a prison. Any dragon who enters cannot leave, unless it descends from both Queen Retribution and Queen Summit. The WildWings are ruled by a Triumvirate — three elected dragons who rule in peaceful rotation, currently Tuatara, Horizon and Monarch — and their abilities vary from family to family, from venom to fire breath to camouflage.

🌈 Ability: varies by dragon — RainWing venom, HiveWing venom, fire breath, camouflage

⛓️ Home: the Dungeon Isle

🔒

The Court of Refuge

Not a tribe but a palace — the Court of Refuge, created by the SeaWing animus Waterfall, where the dragons of the Dungeon Isle live. Twenty-five metal SharpWings, enchanted sentinels created by the SkyWing animus Precipice, guard the island and record the names of every prisoner. The SharpWings sort prisoners into numbered categories, and the higher the category, the more closely the sentinels watch — which is why Sora, in category 10, is under constant guard. Arc 4 is set here.

🔒 Ability: none — just centuries of grudges

🏝️ Home: the Dungeon Isle

World of Pyrrhia

The planet the dragons call home.

🌍 Pyrrhia

A dragon-shaped continent of several biomes. The Ice Kingdom forms its head, the Kingdom of Sand its chest, the Kingdom of the Sea its tail, the Mud Kingdom its back, the Rainforest Kingdom its hind leg and the Sky Kingdom its "wing". The Claws of the Clouds Mountains run through the middle of the continent, and Jade Mountain is the largest peak in the range.

🐛 Pantala

The second continent: a dragon-shaped land of grasslands, savannas, rivers and sinkholes, once covered in forests. The Hives are its most prominent landmarks, and the LeafSilk Kingdom spreads across its southern half.

✨ Animus Magic

A rare genetic gift that lets a dragon enchant objects, plants, animals and even other dragons to do as it pleases. It has been recorded in five of the seven Pyrrhian tribes, and never in Pantala. Darkstalker had it — and it came with a terrible price.

🕯️ Humans

Dragonkind calls them animals. The humans of Pyrrhia have history with the dragons — and the whole dragon-slayering era that gave us the Legend of the Dragonslayer is really a human story.

🩸 A history of war

Thousands of years ago dragons burned human settlements to the ground. Three thousand years ago Queen Falcon was assassinated, and the LeafWings and BeetleWings were framed for it and driven into exile — some to Pantala, and many others confined to the Dungeon Isle. Then, twenty years before Arc 1, a human killed the SandWing queen and started the War of SandWing Succession.

🔮 The Prophecy

"When the war has lasted twenty years... the dragonets will come. Five eggs to hatch on brightest night, five dragons born to end the fight. Darkness will rise to bring the light. The dragonets are coming..." The dragonets were raised believing they were the chosen ones who would end the war. It turned out the whole prophecy was a lie — but it still came true, and the five of them saved Pyrrhia anyway.

Why the Tribes Matter

Each tribe is a whole culture, not a colour scheme. Here's what makes them click.

🧠

Every tribe has a dependency

MudWings have thick, armoured scales, can hold their breath for an hour and breathe fire when they're warm enough. SandWings have scales that radiate warmth and can live on very little food and water. SeaWings breathe and see underwater. RainWings get more than half their energy from the sun. IceWings thrive in subzero cold and blinding light. Nobody can thrive everywhere — and that shapes every alliance.

👑

Every tribe has a ruler problem

Some tribes have queens, some have assemblies, some have a Triumvirate that rotates power, and the HiveWings spent an entire arc under the mind control of a plant. Succession here is never settled — it's a running fight, and dragons will fight it.

🌗

Hybrids are the interesting ones

Sunny is SandWing–NightWing. Darkstalker was NightWing–IceWing. Aurora is IceWing–SkyWing, and Mulberry is SkyWing–LeafWing–NightWing. A hybrid dragon can do things neither parent could — and usually belongs nowhere at all.
