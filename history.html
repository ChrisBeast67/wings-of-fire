The History of Pyrrhia

5,012 years of fire, war and prophecy — from the Scorching to the newest arc.

Dragons count time in AS — After Scorching. The Scorching is the year the dragons overthrew humanity on Pyrrhia and became the dominant species. Everything below is dated in AS, and every entry is sourced from the books and the official guide.

The Eras

Nine eras of Pyrrhian history, from the fall of the human empires to the mysterious theft of an egg.

🔥
Before 0 AS

The Age of Humans

For millions of years humans were the dominant lifeform on Pyrrhia. They organised themselves into advanced, warring empires with sprawling cities, and dragons were rare and usually solitary.

Then the empires started stealing dragon eggs to raise as weapons. The experiments were clumsy and brutal — dragons were hurt trying to control them — and it escalated until the dragons united under one leader and burned the human cities to the ground. That retaliation is the story the dragons tell.

Human accounts say the dragons attacked unprovoked. Either way, the human empires collapsed, and human settlements have never grown large again.

⚡
0 AS

The Scorching

The dragons of Pyrrhia overthrew humanity in a single act of retribution and became the continent's dominant species. This is Year Zero — the year every dragon date is counted from.

After the Scorching came five hundred years of constant warfare as the tribes formed, chose their queens, and reshaped the land. The desert of the Kingdom of Sand was formed during this period.

One small group of humans fled overseas to Pantala, led by a man called Cottonmouth — who, on the other continent, discovered the breath of evil. What happened to him and his companion Freedom is the long shadow that hangs over the third arc.
🏝️
~2,000 AS

The Founding of Pantala and the Dungeon Isle

Three thousand years ago, the NightWing and SkyWing queens Queen Falcon and Queen Hibiscus were neighbours — and two of their Queen's Claws, Darkling and Lemur, conspired to kill Falcon.

Her death was deliberately framed on the LeafWings and BeetleWings. Some LeafWings, BeetleWings and others fled Pyrrhia entirely and made it to Pantala; the rest were imprisoned on the Dungeon Isle. A SkyWing queen named Summit took the throne and split the abandoned territory with Hibiscus.

The dragons who reached Pantala found the continent under the total control of the othermind — and nearly wiped the breath of evil out before they understood what it was connected to.

The Dungeon Isle is where the Court of Refuge would later be built and enchanted by the animus dragon Waterfall, guarded by twenty-five metal SharpWings created by Precipice more than three thousand years ago.
🌑
~3,006 AS

Darkstalker's Time

Two thousand years ago the NightWing Darkstalker hatched on the brightest night, receiving the powers of the three moons — animus magic, mind-reading and prophecy — and leaving his sister Whiteout to hatch a day later because he refused to help her.

He nearly destroyed everything. He enchanted an invisible shield around the Night Kingdom that killed every IceWing who touched it. He trapped his own father, Arctic, in a spell of obedience. And he started a war between the IceWings and NightWings that has never really healed.

In the end it took the NightWing queen Vigilance, the seer Clearsight, and the SeaWing Fathom to stop him. Clearsight took his scroll and tricked him into accepting a bracelet that put him to sleep for two thousand years.

Clearsight flew to the Lost Continent, fell in love with the BeetleWing Sunstreak, and began writing down her visions in a book to protect the tribes of Pantala. That book — the Book of Clearsight — is still there today, guarded in the Temple of Clearsight.
🌳
~4,962 AS

The Tree Wars

Fifty years before the third arc, Queen Wasp of the HiveWings claimed that Clearsight wanted her to rule every tribe of Pantala. It was a lie she had made up — but the Book of Clearsight's authority was enough.

Queen Monarch of the SilkWings stepped down and handed Wasp her crown. Queen Sequoia of the LeafWings asked to read the Book of Clearsight for herself to check. Wasp refused. The war followed.

A peace summit meant to infect Wasp with the breath of evil backfired and instead gave her the power to control her own subjects. The LeafWings lost, and came close to being wiped out entirely. Wasp ordered the trees cut down and burned, because she believed the LeafWings drew their power from them. Any mention of trees was banned in HiveWing society.

The survivors fled into the Poison Jungle and split into two groups — the PoisonWings, who wanted the war restarted, and the SapWings, who wanted peace. Their descendants meet as rivals in The Poison Jungle.
🏜️
~4,992 AS

The War of SandWing Succession

Twenty years before the first arc, a SandWing palace was broken into by three humans — Heath, Rose and Stone. Queen Oasis chased them and was killed by Stone in the process, though Heath took the credit.

Her sister Blister stole the SandWing treasure. In the chaos that followed, Burn rallied the SkyWings and took the IceWings' support, while Blister's enemies closed ranks around Blaze. The War of SandWing Succession began, and the Talons of Peace were founded to end it another way.

The war lasted roughly eighteen years and killed some of the series' best-known dragons, including Burn — killed by a dragonbite viper — and Blister, killed by the Eye of Onyx. It ended with Thorn taking the SandWing throne.
🌟
~5,005 AS

The Brightest Night

Seven years before the first arc, a comet was due to strike Pyrrhia, and every dragon knew that dragonets hatched on the brightest night would be special. The NightWings' Morrowseer and Battlewinner used the occasion to invent a prophecy: five dragonets would hatch and end the war.

On the night itself, Clay, Glory, Starflight, Tsunami and Sunny hatched, each guarded by a dragon sworn to kill them. Asha died delivering Clay's egg. Hvitur was killed for stealing the prophesied SkyWing egg and Burn threw it off the mountain. Scarlet forced Peril to destroy the SkyWing eggs, but one hatchling escaped. Webs stole Tsunami's egg. Morrowseer took Starflight's egg.

The prophecy was never real. It was a scheme to seize the rainforest — and when the dragonets escaped their cave, Morrowseer had them killed. Starflight found that out for himself, and that is the moment the series turns.
🐉
~5,011 AS

Arc 1 — The Dragonets of Destiny

Five dragonets hatch in a cave, raise themselves, and are captured by Queen Scarlet for the SkyWing games. They escape, meet Peril, and race across Pyrrhia: the Mud Kingdom, the Summer Palace of the SeaWings, the Rainforest Kingdom, and the volcanic NightWing island.

When the volcano erupts and Morrowseer is killed by it, Starflight learns the prophecy was a lie. Glory takes the RainWing throne. Ruby takes the SkyWing throne. Thorn takes the SandWing throne. And a comet destroys Darkstalker's bracelet in the distance, waking him after two thousand years.

At the end of the arc, the dragonets plan to open a school where every tribe can learn together. That school becomes Jade Mountain Academy.
🎓
~5,012 AS

Arc 2 — Jade Mountain

Jade Mountain Academy opens for its first thirty-five students, split into winglets: the Jade, Gold and Silver Winglets. Moonwatcher arrives and is contacted inside her own head by the newly-awakened Darkstalker.

Darkstalker begins granting NightWings superpowers, becomes their king at the old Night Kingdom, and sends a plague to the IceWings that kills Queen Glacier — handing the throne to her daughter Snowfall. Peril learns that her father is Chameleon, and Winter uncovers that the masked Pyrite is really Hailstorm.

It ends at the Battle of Jade Mountain, ended by an empathy spell built by Qibli and Turtle — and with Darkstalker turned into Peacemaker by Kinkajou using pieces of his own enchanted scroll.

🦋
~5,012 AS

Arc 3 — The Lost Continent

On Pantala, Luna is captured by HiveWings and sent to the flamesilk cavern. Blue, Cricket, Swordtail and Sundew rescue her, and she is washed up on Pyrrhia instead. Meanwhile the group discovers the Chrysalis — a SilkWing rebellion planned for years.

They learn the truth about the othermind: three woven minds — Freedom (once called Lizard), Cottonmouth, and the breath of evil — which came from Pyrrhia in the first place, invented by an ancient human emperor who wanted to control dragons. Luna severs the sentient skeletons of Freedom and Cottonmouth, and the LeafSilk Kingdom is formed.

Snowfall sends the Pantalan refugees to Sanctuary and vouches for them at the queen's council. Representatives from every tribe are sent to Pantala — and ambushed days after leaving Pyrrhia.
⚓
~5,012 AS

Arc 4 — The Forgotten Isles Prophecy

The Hybrid Prince opens with two MudWings, Umber and his sister Sora, fleeing Jade Mountain after Sora destroys the history cave trying to kill Icicle. On the way to the Dungeon Isle, Umber is nearly eaten by a kraken and is saved by Mulberry — a SkyWing–LeafWing–NightWing hybrid and the only dragon who can cross the Barrier.

They explore the Court of Refuge, where prisoners are sorted into numbered categories and twenty-five metal SharpWings record every name. Beryl and Snakeroot, who lost an election to the Triumvirate of Tuatara, Horizon and Monarch, rule as prisoners now instead.

Umber and Mulberry discover a cave enchanted by Precipice holding two unhatched eggs. Six months later one hatches: Harmony. Then a month after that, seven queens and several others — Snowfall, Thorn, Ruby, Sequoia, Wasp, Glory and Kinkajou — mysteriously vanish, and an unknown dragon steals the second egg.

Why the Dates Matter

Pyrrhia's history is the reason the series feels like a world instead of a series of adventures.

🕰️

The Prophecy Was a Lie

Morrowseer and Battlewinner invented the Dragonet Prophecy to seize the rainforest. The poem promises "five dragons born to end the fight" and that "darkness will rise to bring the light" — it never says the world will end, and no prophecy was ever genuine.

🌑

History Repeats

The Scorching, the Tree Wars, the War of SandWing Succession and the wars of the main series all follow the same shape: one power frames another for a crime, and the frame holds for decades. The heroes of arc one inherit a lie, and arc two is about learning who wrote it.

🌿

The Past Never Left

Cottonmouth's breath of evil came from Pyrrhia in the first place. The Talon Peninsula sank into the sea under Darkstalker's magic, and the Court of Refuge was built on a prison. Every "new" problem in this world has a three-thousand-year-old root.
