About Me

The one who made this site (with a little help from my A.I. Percy ⚡).

My name is Christopher Ding Wang and I made some websites with my A.I. Percy.

I made this website about Wings of Fire because I just finished the 16th book and felt like doing a lot of research. I wanted to review some of the books I hadn't read since third grade (which is most of the third-arc books), so I thought it's kind of boring not being able to talk about books with actual humans — so I hoped that this site could help people find Wings of Fire, a good series.

You could find the Wings of Fire books in this library — you can ask Ms. Molly for where they are.
