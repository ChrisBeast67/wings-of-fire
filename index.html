The Four Arcs

Sixteen main books, five Winglets, two Legends — and one prophecy tying it all together.

I

The Dragonet Prophecy

Five dragonets raised in secret beneath the Combe. The Dragonet Prophecy says they will hatch on the brightest night, unite the tribes and end the fight — “Darkness will rise to bring the light.”

  • The Dragonet Prophecy (2012)
  • The Lost Heir (2013)
  • The Hidden Kingdom (2013)
  • The Dark Secret (2013)
  • The Brightest Night (2014)
II

The Jade Mountain Prophecy

Dragons of every tribe go to school at Jade Mountain Academy. Meanwhile, an ancient animus dragon wakes up in the ruins of the lost city of night — and starts to scheme.

  • Moon Rising (2014)
  • Winter Turning (2015)
  • Escaping Peril (2015)
  • Talons of Power (2016)
  • Darkness of Dragons (2017)
III

The Lost Continent Prophecy

Pantala, the continent of silk and leaf, where a HiveWing queen uses a mind-controlling plant to bend an entire tribe to her will. Five very different dragons dive in to stop her.

  • The Lost Continent (2018)
  • The Hive Queen (2018)
  • The Poison Jungle (2019)
  • The Dangerous Gift (2021)
  • The Flames of Hope (2022)
IV

The Forgotten Isles Prophecy

The newest arc, and the first of a planned three-book arc — two MudWings on the run from Jade Mountain Academy, taken in by the Court of Refuge on the Dungeon Isle. New dragons, new danger.

  • The Hybrid Prince (2026)
  • More to come…

Meet the Dragonets

The five who were chosen — whether they wanted to be or not.

🐢

Clay

MudWing

Big, loyal, kind-hearted MudWing with massive brown wings and a fireproof shell. The biggest of the dragonets — and the one who nearly went through with a monstrous plan to kill the queen.

🦎

Tsunami

SeaWing

A fierce, hot-headed SeaWing who returns to the Kingdom of the Sea to claim the throne that was stolen from her as an egg. She is the dragonets' boldest swimmer.

🌧️

Glory

RainWing

Elegant, vain, and secretly the best fighter in the group. Raised by the RainWings of the Rainforest Kingdom, Glory is sharper and far more dangerous than anyone expects — and she has venom.

🦇

Starflight

NightWing

Smart, curious, and a little too clever for his own good. A NightWing blinded by a volcanic eruption, Starflight maps the world, reads human books, and survives on sheer brainpower.

🌵

Sunny

SandWing–NightWing hybrid

Small, fierce, and hopeless about her wings. Daughter of SandWing queen Thorn, Sunny is the sand-scarred fighter of the group and always the last to give up.
