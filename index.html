The Four Arcs

Sixteen main books, five novellas, two legends — and one prophecy tying it all together.

I

The Dragonet Prophecy

Five dragonets raised in secret beneath the Combe. Prophecy says one of them will end the SandWing war of succession — and that one of them will destroy the world.

  • The Dragonet Prophecy (2012)
  • The Lost Heir (2013)
  • The Hidden Kingdom (2013)
  • The Dark Secret (2013)
  • The Brightest Night (2014)
II

The Jade Mountain Prophecy

Dragons of every tribe go to school at Jade Mountain Academy. Meanwhile, an ancient animus dragon stirs under the mountain and starts to scheme.

  • Moon Rising (2014)
  • Winter Turning (2015)
  • Escaping Peril (2015)
  • Talons of Power (2016)
  • Darkness of Dragons (2017)
III

The Lost Continent Prophecy

Pantala, the continent of bugs and silk, ruled by a HiveWing queen with mind-control powers. Five very different dragons dive in to stop her.

  • The Lost Continent (2018)
  • The Hive Queen (2018)
  • The Poison Jungle (2019)
  • The Dangerous Gift (2021)
  • The Flames of Hope (2022)
IV

The Forgotten Isles Prophecy

The newest arc — two MudWings on the run from Jade Mountain Academy, taken in by the Court of Refuge. New dragons, new danger.

  • The Hybrid Prince (2026)
  • Two more to come…

Meet the Dragonets

The five who were chosen — whether they wanted to be or not.

🐢

Clay

MudWing

Big, loyal, kind-hearted MudWing with a shell like an oak tree. The strongest of the dragonets — and the one who almost went through with a monstrous plan to kill the queen.

🦇

Glory

NightWing

Elegant, vain, and secretly the best fighter in the group. Glory is a NightWing who never met a dragon she couldn't outfly.

🐦

Starflight

SkyWing

Smart, curious, and a little too clever for his own good. Starflight maps the world and survives on sheer brainpower.

🌵

Sunny

SandWing

Small, fierce, and hopeless about her wings. Sunny is the sand-scarred fighter of the group and always the last to give up.

🦎

Dragonet

MudWing

Nobody knows who Dragonet really is. A secret the whole prophecy depends on — and a mystery that changes everything.
